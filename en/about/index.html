About Tekmerion

Tekmerion (from the Greek τεκμήριον, conclusive proof) builds software for people who have to make decisions and defend them. The company is under formation in Athens, Greece.

Our mission

To give institutions, professionals and students the kind of evidence that used to require a five-figure terminal licence: sourced, tested and provable, running on their own computers and on public data.
