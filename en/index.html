Sealed in Bitcoin

Every report, record and forecast is fingerprinted and timestamped in the Bitcoin blockchain with OpenTimestamps. Anyone can verify that it existed, unchanged, on that date.

Used in: Navigator · Proxenos LinkLab · Phryktoria · TROY · Siege Desk

Backtested

Forecasts are tested on history, point-in-time, before they are shown, then scored against named benchmarks once the outcome is known. Models that fail are withheld.

Used in: Chresmos · Phryktoria · Synedrion · TROY · Siege Desk

How we work

01

Every number has a source.

Figures carry their origin on the page. Where the data cannot support a figure, we show a dash, not an estimate.

02

Every forecast is tested before it is shown.

Point-in-time backtests, named benchmarks and public scoring. Models that fail validation are withheld.

03

Every record is permanent and provable.

Reports and records are timestamped in the Bitcoin blockchain with OpenTimestamps, so anyone can verify what was published and when.

04

Your data stays with you.

Most products run on the user's own computer, on free public data, with keys kept locally. Sources that refuse automated readers are never bypassed.

Questions and answers

What is Tekmerion?

Tekmerion is a company under formation in Athens, Greece, that builds market intelligence, forecasting and risk analytics software. Its twelve products show their sources, test their own forecasts and keep provable records.

Who is Tekmerion software for?

Banks, funds and professional investors; energy traders and shipping companies; ministries, diplomats and analysts; universities and students; and property buyers and professionals in Greece and Cyprus.

How are the forecasts tested?

Every forecast is backtested point-in-time, compared with named benchmarks such as the random walk, and scored with Brier score, log-loss and calibration once the outcome is known. Models that fail validation are withheld.

What does sealed in Bitcoin mean?

A fingerprint of each report, statement or forecast is timestamped in the Bitcoin blockchain with OpenTimestamps. Anyone can then verify that the record existed in exactly that form on that date.

Is this investment advice?

No. The products are for research, education and business analysis. Nothing on this site or produced by the products is investment advice.

How can I try a product?

Request a demo. Tekmerion runs pilots with banks, funds, energy and shipping firms, public bodies and universities, using your own data where possible.
