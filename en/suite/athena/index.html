ATHENA Glaux Intelligence

News intelligence, with every source one click away.

A public news-intelligence site in English, Greek and French: events confirmed by independent outlets, a risk reading for each section and country, and a trail back to every original source.

Evidence

≈6,700pages in the sitemap
3languages
10 minpublishing cycle
2+independent outlets per event

How it works

An event is published only when at least two independent outlets report it, and every box on the site leads to the original reports. The site publishes in ten-minute cycles, rebuilds every page each hour and publishes a breaking event at once. Sections cover geopolitics, economy, trade, energy, technology and business, with country pages and a Maritime desk for the chokepoints that carry global trade. Athena Signals records how risk moves by section and compares today with similar past days.
