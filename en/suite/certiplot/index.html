How it works

A page for every region, regional unit and municipality of Greece and every district and municipality of Cyprus, with recorded sales by property type, planning coefficients, flood-risk land, census figures and a list of what to check there. Twelve buyer guides, a purchase-cost calculator and 99 rules in force checked against official text. Prices shown are official values, not market prices. Information is free to buyers and sellers; a subscription app serves agents, civil engineers and surveyors.
