Wealth stewardship education and preparation

Logoi Oikonomias

Be an informed principal before you meet the adviser.

A private preparation platform for people who receive, inherit or create substantial wealth: it helps them say what the resources are for, what they know and what they still need to ask, before they engage a wealth manager, lawyer, accountant or tax adviser.

Evidence

16book modules turned into exercises
4entry routes
10questions in the adviser-selection toolkit
0investment recommendations given

How it works

It joins the book's philosophy of stewardship to short lessons in economics, finance, law, tax and governance, private exercises and records, and a briefing the user controls and can take to any professional. Its central document is a living Oikonomikos Logos: the user's current purposes, knowledge, commitments, uncertainties and questions. The first release is limited to education and preparation: it gives no investment recommendations, manages no portfolios and executes no transactions, and no one is stopped from exporting their work or seeking a professional at any time. Private entries stay private unless the user chooses to share them. The first market is Greece, with an EU-level educational overview.

In development. An educational and preparation tool; it is not investment, legal or tax advice.
