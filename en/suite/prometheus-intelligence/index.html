How it works

Its Gas Balance page sets daily European supply by route (Norway, North Africa, TAP, TurkStream, LNG send-out) against power burn and weather, and projects storage to the EU's 90% filling target with probability bands taken from every past weather year. Greek entry points, outages, an LNG fleet view built on live ship positions and preset shocks complete the desk. Every data connector carries its licence flag, and a commercial mode switches off sources that may not be used commercially. It is a research tool: it has no broker connection and routes no orders.
