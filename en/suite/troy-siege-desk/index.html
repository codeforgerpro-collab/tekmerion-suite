How it works

Forecasts are built walk-forward with a purge, so no training row can see the future, and intervals come from the empirical distribution of past out-of-sample errors rather than an assumed curve. Every number stands next to its baselines (random walk, climatology and uniform); when the model does not beat the random walk, the desk says so. Calls are pre-registered in a SHA-256 hash chain and anchored in the Bitcoin blockchain with OpenTimestamps, then scored automatically when their window closes. Nothing is ever edited. The desk is honest about itself: over 303 weeks its model has not beaten the random walk, and it says so on screen. It rests on five published papers and the original research code, run unchanged. News and the implied-volatility gauge follow the market chosen.
