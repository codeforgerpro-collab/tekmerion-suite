Methodology

Our products differ in subject but share one method: show the source, test the claim, keep the record.

01

Every number has a source.

Figures carry their origin on the page. Where the data cannot support a figure, we show a dash, not an estimate.

02

Every forecast is tested before it is shown.

Point-in-time backtests, named benchmarks and public scoring. Models that fail validation are withheld.

03

Every record is permanent and provable.

Reports and records are timestamped in the Bitcoin blockchain with OpenTimestamps, so anyone can verify what was published and when.

04

Your data stays with you.

Most products run on the user's own computer, on free public data, with keys kept locally. Sources that refuse automated readers are never bypassed.

Provenance

Every figure is traceable to a named public source, shown on the page where it is used. Free, official and verified sources come first; each data connector carries its licence terms, and a commercial mode switches off any source that may not be used commercially. Sources that refuse automated readers are shown as blocked and are never bypassed.

Forecasts that face the evidence

Forecasts are produced only by methods tied to published work, and are backtested point-in-time, without knowledge the forecaster could not have had. Each is compared with named benchmarks (climatology, persistence and the crowd) and scored with Brier score, log-loss and calibration once outcomes are known. Statistical tests such as Diebold–Mariano and Pesaran–Timmermann, bootstrap intervals and Holm correction guard against luck and hindsight.

Code locked to the paper

Where a product implements a paper, a golden test contract fixes the numerical core to it, so the software cannot drift away from the method it claims to follow. Methodology-conformance reports document the match.

Permanent, provable records

Reports, statements and forecasts are hashed and timestamped in the Bitcoin blockchain through OpenTimestamps. Anyone can verify, without trusting us, that a record existed in its exact form on a given date.

Privacy by design

Most products run on the user's own computer. Keys stay local and visible to their owner; nothing is sent to us. Where a hosted edition is offered, it follows GDPR and collects only what the service needs.

Academic foundation

Navigator, Proxenos LinkLab, Chresmos and Synedrion are built on the work and methodology of Professor Dimitrios Thomakos, co-founder of Tekmerion; Synedrion with Dr. Sophia Tarani. Navigator, Chresmos and Synedrion have been donated to the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens for teaching and research. Phryktoria is the early-warning companion to Chresmos.
