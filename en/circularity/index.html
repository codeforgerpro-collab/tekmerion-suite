Circular engineering and sustainability

Every Tekmerion product starts as research and ends as production software. We reuse what is already proven, so each new application costs less to build, to run and to trust.

Four loops that keep value in use

01

Research to production

Methods proven in university research become shared engines: data connectors, backtesting, provenance and timestamping. A new professional application is assembled from verified parts instead of being written from zero, so it reaches deployment sooner and at lower cost.

02

Production back to research

What we learn in production returns to teaching and research. Navigator, Chresmos and Synedrion have been donated to the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens, where students and researchers build on them.

03

Local by design

Most products run on the user's own computer, so there is less hosted infrastructure to build, power and secure, and no data to move. Where a hosted edition exists, it collects only what the service needs.

04

Public data, read once, cited always

We use official and public sources with their licence terms and show the source beside each figure. Code is locked to the published method by golden tests, so a result does not have to be re-derived or re-checked each time a product changes.

What this means for your organisation

Faster deployment

Verified components shorten the path from a proven idea to a working professional application.

Lower total cost

Shared engines mean maintenance, testing and security work are done once and benefit every product.

Auditable results

Every figure has a source, every forecast is backtested, every record can be timestamped and checked.

Less to run

Local-first software reduces the servers, transfers and accounts an organisation has to manage.

What we do not claim

We do not publish carbon or energy figures, because we have not yet measured them across our products. When we have measured numbers, we will publish them here with the method used. Until then, the claims on this page are about reuse of work, code and data, which you can inspect in our methodology and product pages.
