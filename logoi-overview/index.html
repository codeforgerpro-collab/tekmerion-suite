Logoi Oikonomias

Λόγοι Οικονομίας

A private preparation platform for people who receive, inherit or create substantial wealth. It helps them say what the resources are for, what they know and what they still need to ask, before they engage a wealth manager, lawyer, accountant or tax adviser.

Based on the book by Professor Dimitrios Thomakos · Lead Engineer: Antonios Vlachos

The gap

Financial education explains instruments. Family governance explains relationships. Few tools connect them.

Many people walk into a first meeting with an adviser without a clear view of what they own or may inherit, what they owe, how the adviser is paid, or who in the family takes part in decisions. Logoi Oikonomias gives them a private place to work that out first, so the professional relationship starts informed and the user stays in charge of it.

Its promise is better preparation. It does not promise returns, wealth preservation, regulatory compliance or the end of family disagreement.

Who it is for

Four ways in, one document at the end

I may inherit or assume family responsibility

Separate what is owned from what may be transferred, and plan a respectful conversation with those currently responsible.

I have received or created significant wealth

Map resources, obligations and concentrations, and avoid mistaking a first meeting for a commitment to invest.

I want to prepare for a family conversation

A staged guide covering history, intentions, practical facts, hopes and follow-up. Taking part is voluntary.

I am preparing to meet a professional

Questions to ask, documents to gather and a briefing the user edits and chooses what to share.

What the user builds

A living Oikonomikos Logos

The central document is assembled from the user's own entries: what they have received or may receive, what they know and need to learn, what the resources are for, their decision principles and conflicts, who they may owe an account to, and their next steps and review date. Every section is editable, versioned and exportable, including as an unfinished draft.

The professional briefing

A better first conversation

The user reviews a briefing, removes anything they do not want to share, and takes it to any professional. Every statement is labelled as user-provided.

Purpose
What the resources should make possible, the time horizons in mind and purposes that may conflict.
Resources and obligations
Optional high-level categories, liquidity needs, business or property interests, and what is unknown or unverified.
People and decisions
Who owns or controls what, who should take part, and where authority is uncertain.
Questions
Services, authorisation, costs, conflicts, custody, legal and tax coordination, reporting and next steps.
Limitations
User-provided information, not verified by the platform and not a suitability assessment.

Choosing an adviser

Ten questions every user can take into the room

  1. Which services are you authorised to provide, and where can I verify that?
  2. Are you advising, managing, executing, holding assets or coordinating others?
  3. How are you paid, including commissions or payments from third parties?
  4. Which products and providers can you consider, and what limits apply?
  5. What information do you need, and how will you protect it?
  6. What are the estimated total costs and the main assumptions?
  7. Who holds the assets, how are they reported, and what happens if a firm fails?
  8. How are complaints, conflicts, termination and transfer handled?
  9. Which matters need a lawyer, accountant or tax adviser?
  10. How will our non-financial objectives and differing family interests be addressed?

For family offices and wealth professionals

Educate the next generation. Receive better-prepared clients.

Family offices can use the platform to prepare younger members. Law firms, wealth managers and private banks can receive a briefing that already states purposes, obligations and open questions.

Private work stays private. An institution never sees a user's journal, family reflections or financial profile because it paid for access. Anything shared is visible only to the people and for the purposes the user selects.

The platform is independent of any provider. It takes no referral fees in this design, and a professional directory is not part of the first release.

Boundaries

Education and preparation, not advice

  • No buy, sell or hold recommendations, personalised asset allocation, or selection of legal entities.
  • No individual tax calculations presented as advice, and no execution or transmission of orders.
  • No custody, portfolio management or account aggregation.
  • No overall readiness score. A descriptive checklist shows what is complete or missing, and never blocks an export or a call to a professional.
  • Urgent deadlines, suspected fraud, coercion or incapacity are flagged so the user seeks qualified help straight away.

First market: Greece, with an EU-level educational overview. Other jurisdictions are added only after local review.
